# galacticmodel: the genulens port, the start-value frame, and how they are tested

**Provenance:** this component derives from genulens (Koshimoto, Baba & Bennett 2021, ApJ 917, 78, "K21"; https://github.com/nkoshimoto/genulens). Cite K21 when you use it.

The Galactic model is ONE model: `model: koshimoto21`, a faithful port of genulens (`koshimoto21.py`; "The genulens port" below). The FFP mass function, linked stars and the evidence normalizer are documented in `src/exozippy/components/star/star.md` ("Stellar mass priors", "Linked stars", "The stellar distance prior"). This file holds the port -- what it reproduces, where it deviates and how it is validated against genulens -- and the numpy frame (`physics.py`) the start-value hints use. Read it before touching `koshimoto21.py`, `physics.py`'s `line_of_sight_basis`, `galactic_xyz` or `polar_velocity`, or any sign in a velocity transform.

**History: `model: simplified` was removed (review 1.7.6, PR #PRNUM).** It was the earlier 3-population model (one exponential thin disk, a thick disk and the E+E_X bar with constant-dispersion Gaussian kinematics, a selectable Chabrier/Salpeter `IMF:` and a MIST-turnoff `present_day:` mass function), kept only for regression comparison once the port became the default. `model: simplified`, `IMF:` and `present_day:` now RAISE at config load, naming the key; `model: koshimoto21` is still accepted, so a config that spells the default out keeps working. Start logp of every shipped galacticmodel example was bit-identical across the removal.

## The start-value frame: one frame for positions and velocities (`physics.py`)

The likelihood is the port's (its own `koshimoto21.line_of_sight_basis`: genulens's `Dlb2xyz` positions and its `(l, b)` velocity projection). `physics.py` is the numpy layer the START VALUES use: `expected_proper_motion` (`mulensing/mulensevent.py`, `background/background.py`) seeds a star's proper motion at a population's mean rotation -- the three-population rotation centers (thin disk 218 km/s, thick disk 170, bulge `Omega r`), a start value, not the prior. A mean velocity in galactocentric polar coordinates `(v_r, v_phi, v_z)` needs a position `(x, y)` (for the polar angle) and a velocity `(v_x, v_y)` in the SAME frame. The frame is:

- **astropy's `Galactocentric`** as configured in `physics.GALACTOCENTRIC_FRAME`: origin at Sgr A*, `R0 = 8.16 kpc`, `z_sun = 25 pc`, solar motion `(10, 243, 7) km/s` (genulens's values; constants.py),
- **with x mirrored** (`physics.MODEL_FRAME_MIRROR = diag(-1, 1, 1)`), applied to positions and velocities alike.

The mirror puts the frame in genulens's orientation (Koshimoto, Baba & Bennett 2021, `Dlb2xyz`): Sun at `x ~ +R0`, `y` toward `l = 90 deg`, `z` toward the NGP, and the Sun's velocity `(vx, vy, vz)sun = (-10, 243, 7) km/s` -- exactly what genulens prints. Three things follow, and they are why this frame and not astropy's unmirrored one:

1. It is genulens's orientation, the one the port's positions are written in.
2. The Sun at `+x` moving `+y` makes `x v_y - y v_x` positive for co-rotation, so `polar_velocity`'s `v_phi` is positive for Galactic rotation everywhere, as every population mean assumes.
3. The origin is Sgr A*, which is genulens's default (`CenSgrA = 1`); genulens translates to it where astropy rotates to it, a difference below 15 pc over the bulge (`test_positions_track_genulens_dlb2xyz`).

`line_of_sight_basis(ra, dec)` returns `(M_rot, v0, sun_xyz, los_hat)`, all from one astropy transform: the velocity map `v = v0 + M_rot @ (K pm_ra d, K pm_dec d, rv)` and the position `sun_xyz + d * los_hat` are both exactly affine, so a linearization at 1 kpc is exact. `M_rot` is orthogonal with `det = -1` (the mirror). `galactic_xyz(d, *basis[2:])` is the position; it is pure arithmetic.

**Do not build a position or a velocity by hand.** Until 2026-10 (review 1.7.1) the position was built by hand in genulens's orientation (`x = R0 - d cos l cos b`) while the velocity was astropy's unmirrored one, so `v_x` entered `v_r` and `v_phi` with the wrong sign: an exactly circular orbit at 4 kpc toward ob140939 got `v_r = +47 km/s`, at `l = 90 deg`, 4 kpc `+172 km/s`, and the Sun itself moved outward at `+10 km/s`. The mismatch was born when the model was integrated (it kept the reference script's positions and took astropy's velocities); the 2026-08 fix that moved the rotation onto `v_phi` and the later matching of the two frames' `R0`/`z_sun`/`v_sun` both left the sign of `x` alone, because the tests of the time compared log-density margins through the mixture, whose hotter branches absorbed the error.

## Tests

`tests/test_galactic_frame.py` checks computed QUANTITIES against astropy, not margins:

| test | what it pins | on the two-frame code |
|---|---|---|
| circular orbits (grid l = 0, +/-5, +/-30, +/-90, 180; b = 0, +/-3; d = 0.5-12 kpc) | `v_r = v_z = 0`, `v_phi = speed` to 1e-6 km/s through the frame's own path, disk speed and bulge `Omega r` | `v_r` up to 172 km/s |
| prior-mean proper motion | `expected_proper_motion` (the start hint) is a circular orbit in astropy, every population | `v_r` 2.6-215 km/s |
| positions | astropy's Galactocentric with x mirrored, 1e-12 kpc; GC and Sun where they belong | within 2 pc (the positions were nearly right; the velocities' x was not) |
| the Sun | a star at d = 0 at rest has `(-10, 243, 7)`, `v_phi = 243`, `v_r = -10` | `v_r = +10` |
| velocity map | equals astropy's full transform (1e-9 km/s) and inverts to machine precision | x component sign-flipped |
| rotation sense | a co-rotating bulge star has `v_phi = +Omega r`, its counter-rotating twin `-Omega r` | 32.16 for 34.62 |

## Findings of the removed model worth keeping

Two measurements made on `model: simplified` explain choices the port inherits; the model itself is gone (above).

- **The bar shape moves the sources (review 1.7.3).** The model first had a plain ellipsoid bar `exp(-r_s/2)`, axes (1.59, 0.42, 0.42) kpc at 25 deg -- an e-folding length of 3.2 kpc along the bar against K21's ~0.9 kpc. Review 7.16.3's prior-predictive check found microlensing SOURCES ~0.9 kpc farther than genulens's (rate-weighted D_S median 9.7 vs 8.7 kpc at Baade's window, same `n D^2` source weighting), and the unweighted source density `n(D) D^2` along Baade's window alone had its median at 8.60 kpc with the old bar against 8.03 with K21's E+E_X bar. The thin and thick disks were not it: genulens's seven sech^2 thin-disk age bins and one exponential layer agree to 5% in number density at |z| = 0.1-0.2 kpc toward Baade.
- **Bar kinematics are the mu_rel gap.** With K21's bar density but constant bar dispersions (120, 100, 80 km/s) and solid-body rotation, Baade's rate-weighted mu_rel median sat 13% below genulens's (KS 0.116); K21's position-dependent dispersions and streaming along the bar, which the port reproduces, close it (the end-to-end table below).

## The genulens port (`koshimoto21.py`)

The galactic model is a faithful port of genulens (Koshimoto, Baba & Bennett 2021, ApJ 917, 78, "K21"; https://github.com/nkoshimoto/genulens, version 2.0.0a4, commit 2cc5870). The goal is numerical EQUIVALENCE with genulens, not an improvement on it: users inherit the community's analyses of K21 and their ways of reweighting it. Every function in `koshimoto21.py` names the genulens function it reproduces, and every constant carries genulens's digits. Do not "fix" a genulens choice in the port (the untilted x in `Dlb2xyz`, the bar dispersions without their x-y covariance, the thin-disk turnoff at each age bin's median age): the port is the reference implementation's twin, and a disagreement with genulens is a porting bug.

Where a gradient sampler forces a deviation it is stated next to the code with its expected size. The planned ones: genulens evaluates the Shu distribution function at the table node below a star (piecewise constant in R and z) and truncates it where it falls below 1% of its peak, and its velocity samplers reject draws above the escape speed (550 km/s disk, 600 km/s bar); the port evaluates the distribution at the star's own (R, z), keeps the tails and does not renormalize for the escape cut (each below ~1% of the density). Remnant masses from the Raithel+18 initial-final mass relation, which has point masses and stationary points, are kernel-smoothed, and the natal kicks (fixed 350/100 km/s, isotropic) are convolved with an isotropic equivalent of the local dispersion.

**Validation is point by point, against genulens's own functions.** `scripts/genulens_reference_driver.cpp` links genulens's `libgenulens_core.a` (built by the user from genulens's source; nothing of genulens is copied here), initializes the model exactly as genulens's sampler does, and prints what genulens's functions return: the start-up normalizations, positions and the 11 component densities (`calc_rho_each`), the velocity building blocks and the moments of genulens's own velocity sampler (`get_vxyz_ran`), the Shu distribution (`calc_PRRg`), the IMF grid, the turnoff tables and the IFMR (`Mini2Mrem`), and the luminosity-function source selection (`fLF_detect`). `scripts/make_genulens_points.py` compiles and runs it on four sight lines (ob140939's, Baade's window with and without the luminosity-function cut, and one where genulens switches the nuclear stellar disk on) and writes `tests/fixtures/genulens_points.json`. `tests/test_koshimoto21.py` holds the port to it: analytic pieces to the fixture's 12 significant digits (the positions, the Sgr A* offset and every density shape -- seven thin-disk age bins, the thick disk, the E+E_X bar and the stellar halo -- already do), tabulated and Monte Carlo pieces to the tolerance each test states. The event-level comparison is the prior-predictive harness below (review 7.16.3), against genulens's own simulator.

genulens's default source weighting is `sqrt(D_S / 8 kpc)` times the main-sequence number density -- no `D_S^2` volume element and no luminosity function -- unless a red-clump extinction `AIrc` switches on its luminosity-function cut. genulens's default weighting moves its D_S median from 8.73 kpc (`gammaDs 2`, `n D_S^2`) to 8.52 kpc toward Baade's window; the port reproduces genulens's source weighting explicitly.

### What `model: koshimoto21` ports today (density and kinematics)

`galacticmodel: {model: koshimoto21}` is the default and the only value (the key may be left out). One spelling, case-sensitive; anything else raises, `simplified` with a message saying it was removed.

- **Branches.** Ten: the seven thin-disk age bins, the thick disk, the E+E_X bar and the stellar halo (`koshimoto21.BRANCHES`), each with genulens's shape (`calc_rho_each`) and its start-up normalization `n0` -- ALL objects, white dwarfs included, which is genulens's LENS density (`N0`; the main-sequence count `N0_MS` is its source density and waits for the source-weighting port). Positions are genulens's `Dlb2xyz` (Sgr A* origin, its tilted z), velocities its `(l, b)` projection with `K = 1000 / KS2MY` (`koshimoto21.line_of_sight_basis`; the `(pm_ra, pm_dec) -> (mu_l, mu_b)` rotation is astropy's).
- **Velocity densities, each normalized** (so `evidence_log_norm` owes only the rv column's m/s Jacobian): disk `v_R ~ N(0, sigU(R))`, `v_z ~ N(0, sigW(R))` with the age-dependent dispersions at each bin's median age, and `v_phi` from genulens's Shu distribution function -- `calc_PRRg` ported exactly (12 digits against genulens), turned into a density in `v_phi` through the exact inverse of `L = v_c(R_g) R_g` on the piecewise-linear rotation curve, and normalized by a shipped `log Z(R, |z|)` table (`koshimoto21_shu_lognorm.npz`, `scripts/make_koshimoto21_tables.py`; within 0.7% of genulens's own normalization, bilinear interpolation error < 0.3%, worst at R < 1 kpc). Bar: genulens's Gaussian in Galactic Cartesian velocity with solid-body rotation, the streaming along the bar and `calc_sigvb`'s dispersions, projected without the x-y covariance as genulens does. Halo: zero-mean Gaussian in `(v_R, v_phi, v_z)`.
- **Deviations, measured** (`tests/test_koshimoto21.py`): genulens draws `f_g` from the table node below a star, the port at the star's own `(R, z)` -- the central `v_phi` quantiles agree with genulens's own sampler to < 8% of the 16-84% width (median 2%; worst at R ~ 1 kpc, where a 100 pc node step is 10% of R); genulens truncates its `f_g` table where P < 1% of the peak, the port keeps the tails; the 550/600 km/s escape-speed rejection is not renormalized. Where genulens returns zero (counter-rotation for the disk, `c <= 0.5`) the port's log density is floored at a finite, very negative value with strictly positive floors, and both forms of genulens's `g(c)` are finite, so no selection carries a NaN (CLAUDE.md).
- **The mass function (genulens's PDMF), per branch.** K21's broken power law (`koshimoto21.log_imf`: exactly the minimum of its three lines, so no branch selection; genulens's grid density to 1e-10) on [0.001, 120] Msun, alive below genulens's PARSEC turnoff `Minidie` -- each thin bin at its median age (the nearest 50-Myr row), the thick disk at 12 Gyr, the halo at 14 Gyr, the bar the EXACT finite mixture over its 0.5-Gyr rows that genulens's per-event age draw N(9, 1) and rounding produce -- and the dead turned into white dwarfs (Kalirai+08), neutron stars and black holes (Raithel+18) by `pdmf.build_population_tables` (0.02 dex smoothing). Normalized by the IMF's integral over the star's logmass support, so with `remnants: true` (default, Q1) every born star is one object and with `false` the branch weight is `n0 F_alive`, exactly genulens's REMNANT 0 rejection. Neutron stars and black holes carry genulens's natal kicks (350 / 100 km/s, isotropic): their velocity density is the closed-form convolution of a shell with an ISOTROPIC Gaussian of the branch's mean and det-preserving dispersion (`koshimoto21.log_kicked_velocity`; disk v_phi dispersion taken as sigU / sqrt 2) -- a stated approximation of genulens's anisotropic base, mass and velocity combined per branch as `log[phi_still p_b(v) + phi_NS p_NS(v) + phi_BH p_BH(v)]`. A star whose age is sampled (MIST) is luminous by its own track (Q4): K21's IMF alone.  `IMF:` and `present_day:` (keys of the removed `simplified` model) raise.
- **Interim, until ported per branch:** the age / [Fe/H] priors are the three-population ones, mapped by `koshimoto21.POPULATION_OF_BRANCH` (thin bins -> thin disk, halo -> thick disk).
- **The nuclear stellar disk.** On sight lines inside genulens's box (|l| < 5, |b| < 2) the mixture has 11 branches: genulens's ND = 3 NSD, its mass density bilinear in log10 on the Sormani+21 moment table (`koshimoto21_nsd.npz`, from genulens's input_files/NSD_moments.dat; to 1e-12 against genulens), with genulens's normalizations and its Gaussian kinematics (an R-z correlation), turnoffs from its "N" rows mixed over its N(7, 1) age draw.  Beyond genulens's table edge (R > 970, |z| > 380 pc), where its density is 0, a 5-pc Gaussian roll-off keeps the log finite (the table there is ~1e-9 of central).
- **Source weighting.** A star a component OBSERVED through a flux limit (`Component.selected_sources`; the microlensing event returns its source) takes genulens's SOURCE density instead of its lens density: the main-sequence count `N0_MS` times genulens's weight, which REPLACES the `D^2` volume element: `source_selection: sqrt_distance` (default; genulens's default, gammaDs = 0.5: `sqrt(D / 8 kpc)`, no `D^2`) or `luminosity_function` (genulens's AIrc mode: `D^2 fLF_b(D)`, the fraction of each branch's I-band luminosity function -- `koshimoto21_lf.npz`, printed by genulens's own make_LFs -- inside `source_i_range` given `source_ai_rc` and genulens's dust law; to 1e-10).  Its mass prior is the LUMINOUS part of its branch's PDMF, normalized over the luminous objects.  The sqrt weight drops `D^2` from the density, so a source's start logp moves by about `-2 ln D` (~ -18 nats at 8 kpc); the position normalizer (`evidence_log_norm`) integrates the same weighted density.
- **Linked stars.** A member whose distance AND velocity are its root's (a binary companion: KMT-2021-BLG-1122L, ob09020, ob170114) contributes its mass term only, inside its root's branch; one with its own velocity, its full normalized term.  A partly linked velocity raises (its conditional has no closed form under the non-Gaussian disk kinematics).
- **Binary lenses (`binaries: true`, genulens's BINARY 1; default false, genulens's default).** For a star a component names as a single lens whose mass may be an unresolved binary's (`Component.unresolved_binary_lenses`; a single-lens microlensing event's lens), the luminous part of its branch's mass function becomes genulens's EFFECTIVE lens mass: its multiplicity 0.196 + 0.255 M, its mass-ratio power laws and half-normal log separations (Koshimoto+20 after Duchene & Kraus 13), body 1 chosen with probability sqrt(M1) / (sqrt(M1) + sqrt(M2)), u0 uniform; a companion projected beyond genulens's wide threshold leaves body 1 as the lens, one inside its close threshold makes the lens the total mass, and a detectable binary is dropped (genulens retries).  Both thresholds scale with the physical Einstein radius, so the density is a table in (log10 M_eff, log10 r0), r0 = sqrt(kappa pi_rel) D_L [AU / sqrt(Msun)] (`koshimoto21_binary.npz`, Monte Carlo of 2e6 primaries, 0.02-dex smoothing; white dwarfs, as in genulens, get no companion).  Against genulens's own BINARY 1 events (Baade, 300k), the effective-mass q10 / q50 / q90 per r0 bin agree to <= 0.03 dex.
- **Compile cost:** the 8 disk components are one broadcast expression (`_DISK_COLS`), not 8 copies; still, the gradient graph takes ~1-2 min to C-compile on a cold PyTensor cache and a few seconds warm.

The 0.9 kpc source-distance offset of the removed 3-population model (review 1.7.3) is gone: it was genulens's `sqrt(D_S)` source weighting against its `D_S^2`, and the bar shape.  The port's mu_rel is heliocentric here (no light curve, no t0_par) where genulens's is geocentric at its default Earth velocity (genulens itself moves its t_E median by < 1% between opposite seasons' Earth velocities).  The prior still shows 14% divergent transitions (review 1.7.4) under the port, so they were never specific to the removed model.
